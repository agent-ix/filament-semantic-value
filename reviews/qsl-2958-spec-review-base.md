## Summary

Ticket: QSL-2958. Reviewed the full FR-119 text and TC-919, including the new ordered-position criteria, against FSL FR-364-AC-15/16 and TC-1902. The ordered-position API, end-specific numeric ordering, grouped uniqueness and refusal ordering are consistent with the FSL contract. The three new criteria marked Test remain untagged in the Quire matrix while TC-919 is explicitly planned/unrun.
